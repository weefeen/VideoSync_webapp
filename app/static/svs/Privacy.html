Privacy

Your video is confidential

Nobody else sees the videos you upload to our server.

You are about to send us a recording of yourself at the piano. That is a personal thing to hand over, so here is exactly what happens to it — in plain words, and short enough to read before you decide.

Nobody else ever sees it Your performance is not published, not shared, not sold, and never used in advertising. It does not appear on this site, on social media, or anywhere else. It is not shown to other users, and it is not passed to any other company.

Why we keep it

To make the software better at its job. Teaching a computer to hear which piece is being played, and to follow a score in time with a performance, takes real recordings to learn from — made on ordinary pianos, in ordinary rooms, with whatever microphone was to hand. That is the part no professional recording library can supply, and it is why your upload is genuinely useful to us.

It is used for that and nothing else.

Your video, and how long you have to fetch it

  • You have 48 hours to download your finished video. The link is in the email we send you when it is ready.
  • After that the link stops working, and the video is put into long-term storage. It is not thrown away — ask us and we will fetch it back for you.

Your email address

We use it to send you your own video, and for nothing else. No newsletters, no marketing, and we do not pass it to anyone. Nothing you type on this site appears in any message we send.

We ask for it because making the video takes several minutes, and up to an hour for a long recording. Rather than have you sit here waiting, we send you a link when it is ready. You will get two messages and no more: one saying we have your recording, one saying it is finished.

You do not have to rely on the email. The address in your browser's bar changes to one that belongs to your video — bookmark it, and you can close this page and come back to it whenever you like.

One thing we would rather say plainly than bury. The scores here are engraved by hand, one at a time, and there are far more Chopin pieces than we have reached. If you play one we do not have yet, we cannot make your video — and when that happens your email address is sent to the person who runs this site, along with the name of the piece, so the score can be engraved and your video made and sent to you afterwards. It goes to us and to nobody else, and nothing you typed goes with it.

Your internet address

There is no account here and no password, so your internet address is the only way we can tell one visitor from another. We use it for two things, and we keep it alongside your upload so that we can.

The first is simply counting. Making a video costs us real machine time, so there is a limit on how many you can ask for in a day — and without a login, counting against your address is the only way to apply that limit instead of asking you to register.

The second is deciding where to put our servers. Your address tells us roughly which country and city a recording came from, and if most of them turn out to come from Europe there is no good reason for the site to be answering from America. That lookup happens on our own machine, against a list we keep here — your address is not sent to anyone to be looked up.

We do not use it to identify you, we do not link it to anything outside this site, and it goes when your recording goes.

Changed your mind?

Reply to the email we sent you and ask us to delete your recording. We will remove it, the video made from it, everything derived from it, the internet address recorded with it, and — if your address reached our own mailbox because we had no score for your piece — that message too. You do not need to give a reason, and there is nothing to argue about.

Please only upload your own playing

Before uploading you are asked to confirm the recording is yours, or that you have permission for it. We would rather you took that seriously than quickly: whoever is playing has just as much say in what happens to a recording of them as you do about yours.

Two small technical things

This page borrows its lettering from Google Like most websites, it loads its typefaces from Google's servers, which means Google can see your internet address when the page opens. We would rather you knew.
Your browser remembers your email address So you are not asked to confirm it twice. It is kept on your own device, never on ours, and clearing your browser's data for this site removes it.

There is no advertising here, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party scripts.

Anything else

Reply to the email we sent you, or find us at weefeen.com. A person reads it.
