Privacy

Your recording is never distributed

That is the short version, and it is the part most people want to know. Your performance is not published, not shown to anyone, not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used to advertise anything. It does not appear on this site, on social media, or anywhere else.

What we hold, and why We keep the video you uploaded, the video we made from it, and a small amount of derived data — the audio's harmonic fingerprint and the map of where each bar falls in time. We keep them for one reason: to make the system better at recognising pieces and following scores. Recordings made at home, on ordinary instruments in ordinary rooms, are exactly what it needs to learn from, and the one thing a library of studio recordings cannot teach it.

Who sees it

Nobody outside Weefeen. The recordings are used to develop and test the software, and that is all they are used for. There is no third party with access to them.

How long things last

  • Your download link works for 48 hours after your video is ready. The address is in the email we send you.
  • After that the video moves to long-term storage. It is not deleted, and we can restore it if you ask.
  • Your recording is kept while we are developing the system.

Your email address

It is used to send you the link to your own video, and for nothing else. You will not receive marketing from us, and the address is not passed to anyone. Nothing you type into this site appears in any message we send.

Deleting your recording

Reply to the email you were sent and say so. We will delete the recording, the video and everything derived from it. You do not have to give a reason.

Two technical things worth stating

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts Which means your browser contacts Google's servers when this page loads, and Google can see your IP address as a result. That is a property of every site that loads fonts this way, and we mention it because it is true rather than because it is unusual.
Your browser remembers your email address So you do not have to confirm it twice. It is stored on your own device, not on our servers, and clearing your browser's data for this site removes it.

There is no analytics on this site, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts of any kind.

Uploading someone else's performance

Please do not. The confirmation before uploading asks whether the recording is yours or you have permission for it, and that question is meant seriously — the person playing has a say in what happens to a recording of them, exactly as you do.

Asking us something

Reply to the email you were sent, or write to us through weefeen.com.
